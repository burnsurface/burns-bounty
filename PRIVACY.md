# Burn’s Bounty Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** The date this Privacy Policy is first published.

The Burn’s Bounty operator handles information to run the Burn’s Bounty Discord bot and its Splinterlands card and DEC raffle features. This policy covers the operator’s handling of information. Discord, Splinterlands, Hive, and Hive Engine have their own practices.

## 1. Information Burn’s Bounty collects

- **Discord account and interactions.** Your Discord user ID, your username or display name where needed, server and channel identifiers, and details of bot interactions you use. These include commands, button selections, raffle entries, card submissions, wishlist requests, and information you send the operator for support.
- **Linked Hive account and transfer data.** Your Hive account name, registration and deposit memos, transaction IDs, timestamps, amounts, sending and receiving account names, and card IDs or ownership information used to verify transfers and process requests.
- **Account and activity records.** Bounty Ledger balances and entries, reservations, deposits, withdrawals, listings, purchased and returned tickets, raffle results, prizes, fees, and statistics used for profiles and leaderboards.
- **Operational records.** Discord message and channel references and records used to deliver or troubleshoot alerts, recover missing records, and prevent duplicate credits.

Account linking uses a DEC transfer. Keep private keys, passwords, and wallet recovery phrases out of bot commands, transfer memos, and support messages.

## 2. How Burn’s Bounty uses information

Burn’s Bounty uses this information to link accounts, verify asset transfers and card ownership, maintain balances and inventory, run raffles, issue refunds and prizes, process withdrawals, show account history, and publish raffle results and rankings. The operator also uses it to investigate disputes, recover failed transactions, prevent duplicate credits and abuse, and meet applicable obligations.

## 3. Who can see information

- **Other server members.** People with access to the relevant channels or views may see Discord names or mentions, card listing details, raffle participation or ticket assignments where the bot displays them, winner announcements, prize amounts, and leaderboard entries.
- **The operator.** The operator can review linked accounts, balances, transaction history, and pending requests to provide support and reconcile activity. The bot sends administrative records to restricted Discord channels when configured. Discord’s server permissions determine who can view those channels.
- **Services used by the bot.** Discord handles interactions, messages, and bot output. The bot queries Hive, Hive Engine, and Splinterlands services or their API providers to verify account, transfer, or card information. Relevant Hive account names, card IDs, or transaction references may be used in those lookups. Information may also be disclosed when required by law.

## 4. Public blockchain records

Hive and Hive Engine transfers can be publicly visible, including account names, amounts, transaction IDs, timestamps, and memos recorded on-chain. A public transfer may let others infer activity associated with a Hive account. Burn’s Bounty can address the records it controls. The operator cannot erase public blockchain records or copies held independently by others.

## 5. Retention and your requests

Burn’s Bounty retains records as needed to operate accounts, settle outstanding assets and transactions, resolve disputes, prevent duplicate credits, and meet applicable obligations. Retention depends on the record and the purpose it serves.

To ask what information Burn’s Bounty holds about you or to request correction, unlinking, or deletion, send a private Discord message to the Burn’s Bounty server owner. Start from the Discord account linked to your profile where possible. The operator may verify that you control the account and will address the records Burn’s Bounty controls promptly, subject to applicable retention obligations. If you have pending withdrawals, open entries, or remaining assets, identify those in your request so the operator can coordinate settlement. Unlinking your Hive account and deleting your data are separate requests.

## 6. Updates and contact

Burn’s Bounty will post material policy changes in the server. For privacy questions or requests, send a private Discord message to the Burn’s Bounty server owner.
