These Terms are between you and the Burn’s Bounty operator. By using the bot, you agree to these Terms. Discord, Splinterlands, Hive, and Hive Engine have their own terms and policies, which apply when you use their services.

## 2. Eligibility and account linking

You must meet the age and location requirements that apply to you and to the activity you enter. Use your own Discord account and link a Hive account you control. Do not use extra accounts to bypass ticket limits or other restrictions. Burn’s Bounty links accounts after verifying the 1 DEC registration payment. This payment is a registration fee. It does not increase your spendable Bounty Balance.

## 3. Deposits and the Bounty Ledger

Your Bounty Ledger tracks credited DEC, assigned cards, and amounts or cards reserved for pending activity. Follow the deposit instructions in `/profile`, including the destination account and exact memo when required. Check the destination, asset, and amount before sending anything. Incorrect or unsupported transfers may require manual review and may be unrecoverable. If a deposit is missing, contact the operator privately with the transaction ID, the sending Hive account, and the amount or card ID. Keep passwords, private keys, and recovery phrases private.

## 4. Raffles, fees, and refunds

- **Entering a raffle.** The raffle post shows the ticket price, prize, number of spots, and any ticket limit. Winners are drawn at random from eligible tickets. Free raffles allow one ticket per person. Other limits appear on the raffle post.
- **Listing a card.** You may submit an eligible card you own in the Bounty Ledger. The card is reserved while queued or listed. Paid card raffles have a seller fee of 5% of ticket sales, rounded up to the next whole DEC and deducted at completion. A free card giveaway has a separate 5 DEC seller fee reserved at submission. That fee is returned if you remove the listing while it is queued or a bot error cancels it. It is kept if the raffle completes, times out, or is canceled after going active.
- **Card listing deposit.** The bot holds the greater of 5 DEC or half the seller fee rounded up. The listing confirmation shows the hold and expected payout before you confirm. The full listing deposit is returned if the raffle completes, you remove the listing while it is queued, or a bot error cancels it. If an active card raffle has unsold tickets after 12 hours, it is canceled and half the listing deposit is returned, rounded up. If an active listing is manually canceled, the listing deposit is forfeited. On cancellation, outstanding paid tickets are refunded to buyers and the card is released to the seller’s Bounty inventory.
- **Returning tickets.** Use the raffle’s return or refund control before the draw while returns are available. Paid ticket refunds go back to your Bounty Ledger. If Burn’s Bounty cancels a raffle, it credits outstanding paid tickets back to the buyers’ ledgers. Completed draws are final except where a verified error requires correction or applicable rules require otherwise.
- **Prizes and seller proceeds.** DEC prizes and seller proceeds are credited to the applicable Bounty Ledger. A winning card is assigned to the winner’s Bounty inventory. To move credited DEC or an available card to your linked Hive account, submit a withdrawal from `/profile`.

## 5. Withdrawals

You can request a withdrawal of available DEC or eligible cards to your linked Hive account. Burn’s Bounty reserves the requested assets while the request is pending. The operator reviews requests and sends approved transfers manually. Timing can vary with verification, asset availability, and network or service outages. Contact the operator if a request appears delayed or incorrect.

Where an account or activity is restricted, contact the operator to resolve any legitimate balance, asset, or pending request. If service is discontinued, Burn’s Bounty will communicate how members can request settlement of eligible remaining assets.

Burn’s Bounty will post material changes in the server and update these Terms. The terms shown when you confirm an entry or listing govern that activity unless a correction or applicable requirement calls for a change. For a missing deposit, payout issue, or other dispute, send a private Discord message to the Burn’s Bounty server owner. Include the relevant raffle number or transaction ID if available.
